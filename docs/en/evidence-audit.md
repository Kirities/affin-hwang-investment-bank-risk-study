## New analysis and its status

The ratio decomposition, entity comparison, reverse-stress grid, and parameter perturbations were implemented and executed for this repository. They are editorial and analytical extensions, not analyses claimed to have appeared in the original source. The empirical inputs remain limited to disclosed capital/RWA observations. The scenario results do not validate internal risk scores or establish predictive accuracy.

## Publication boundary

The source report is retained locally with a SHA-256 fingerprint documented in [references](references.md). Public files exclude direct identifiers, signatures, similarity-check pages, marking forms, and duplicate financial screenshots. No unverified allegations are recast as confirmed institutional misconduct.
